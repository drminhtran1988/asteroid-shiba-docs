## What each one does

* **Pistol** — the better it is, the more ASTRO every find is worth. It does not change CARGO, it does not help her get through a scene, and it does not open new places.
* **Backpack** — decides how much of the loot makes it home. A Backpack below the Pistol leaves part of the loot behind; a Backpack above the Pistol gives no extra.

## The matched set

A **matched set** is a Backpack on the same level as the Pistol. Everything she finds makes it home — the share is **100%**.

The Backpack always costs **20% of the Pistol**, so a matched set is the Pistol price plus a fifth:

These are **model estimates from our own economy model**, not a promise. They are what a matched set returns in ASTRO, converted at 1 ASTRO = 0.001 USDT, until the total equals what the set cost.

**Two columns, because play style is the biggest factor.**

* **Playing every day** — you open the game once per UTC day, so [the Bar](bar.md) takes nothing, the evening correction works and the workshop gets its order. This is the modelled active strategy.
* **Never opening the game** — Shiba still flies, but the Bar takes 25–35% of each day's ASTRO and no orders are given.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Your own result will be faster or slower.** These figures come from one modelled strategy on an average run. What you actually get depends on how you split Valuables and Material, which upgrades the workshop builds and when, how the scenes fall, how often a setback costs you a departure — and on the season's own length. Nothing here is a guaranteed return, a promise of income or a payment schedule.
{% endhint %}

## When the pair is not matched

Only one thing matters, and only in one direction: **how far the Backpack is behind the Pistol.**

| Backpack compared to Pistol | Share of loot delivered |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| same level or higher | **100%** |
| 1 level lower | 95% |
| 2 levels lower | 90% |
| 3 levels lower | 85% |
| each further level | −5% |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**A Backpack behind the Pistol costs you loot on every single expedition.** The share is cut by 5% per level of difference, and it cuts **both ASTRO and CARGO** — so you find more and deliver less, every flight, for as long as the gap is there. Pistol Lv10 with Backpack Lv1 delivers **55%**: almost half of what she digs out stays on the asteroid.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**A Backpack above the Pistol gives you nothing extra.** The share stops at 100% — it never goes above it, and a bigger Backpack does not make a find worth more. Only the Pistol decides what a find is worth. Buying Backpack levels your Pistol hasn't reached yet is money that does no work until the Pistol catches up.
{% endhint %}

The Arsenal shows the current share and the share after the upgrade you are looking at. The game doesn't forbid any combination — how you split your budget between the two is your call.

**You only pay the difference.** The value of the level you already have is credited. You can skip levels: going from Pistol Lv1 to Lv3 costs 225 − 50 = **175 USDT**.

A **Pistol Lv0** has no credit value: from Lv0, Pistol Lv1 costs the full **50 USDT**.

## Buying

If you don't have a Backpack yet, your first purchase is **Backpack Lv1** — it unlocks the rest of the Arsenal.

1. Pick the item and the level. The Arsenal shows the full price, the credit and what you pay.
2. Pay the exact amount in **USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)** to the address shown.
3. The upgrade arrives when the payment is confirmed.

You can have **one open payment at a time**. If a payment expires unpaid, the Arsenal lets you get a new address, or go back and choose different gear.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Send funds only to the address shown for your current payment. Never send to an old, expired address — money sent there needs a manual check by support.
{% endhint %}

Your referrer earns a commission on every Arsenal purchase. See [Rates & active referrals](../referral/rates.md).
