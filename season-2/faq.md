# Season 2 FAQ

**Is Season 2 live?**
The **prelaunch is live now** — you can move in and get ready. The start of the season will be announced in the [official channels](../reference/links.md).

**What do I need to subscribe to?**
Coming from Season 1: [@TheMasked\_Dev](https://t.me/TheMasked_Dev). New players: the announcements channel [@asteroidshiba\_p2e](https://t.me/asteroidshiba_p2e) and the chat [@asteroidshiba\_game](https://t.me/asteroidshiba_game). See [Joining Season 2](joining.md).

**How do I join the Community Drop?**
Leave the hashtag from our [pinned post on X]({PIN}) in a reply under that post. See [Community Drop](community-drop.md).

**Can I own an asteroid in Season 2?**
No. The Belt is occupied — Shiba raids asteroids and flies home with what she can carry. See [The Occupied Belt](story.md).

**Do I need to launch expeditions myself?**
No. Five departures a day are in the schedule, and Shiba flies on her own. Open the game once per UTC day to make your decisions and keep her out of [the Bar](bar.md).

**Can Shiba die or lose her gear?**
No. A setback ends the expedition and she skips the next departure, but she always comes home, and gear never breaks.

**Does a better Pistol protect her from setbacks?**
No. Getting through a scene depends on the Helmet, Suit, Scanner, Boots and Rope. The Pistol decides how much ASTRO her finds are worth.

**What happens if my Backpack is lower than my Pistol?**
Part of the loot stays on the asteroid: −5% per level of difference. See [The Arsenal](arsenal.md).

**Can I withdraw ASTRO in Season 2?**
Yes, the same way as before: $10–$150 per withdrawal, one per 24 hours, one open request at a time, processed within 72 hours. See [Payments & Withdrawals](../payments/overview.md).

**Can I sell or withdraw CARGO?**
No. CARGO is workshop material only. ASTRO is the currency.

**Can I buy the Helmet, Suit or other gear?**
No. The base builds them from CARGO — up to fifteen upgrades a season, opened by calendar. See [Gear & the Workshop](gear-workshop.md).

**What happens to my Season 1 Scanner?**
Its level becomes your Pistol level. See [Joining Season 2](joining.md).

**I'm new. Is it free?**
The starter kit is free. Its Pistol Lv0 works for 30 days; after that you need Pistol Lv1 (50 USDT) to keep flying. The Backpack stays yours. On the free kit you can withdraw up to $20 in total; Pistol Lv1 removes the limit. See [Joining Season 2](joining.md).

**Do referrals work in Season 2?**
Yes — same rates, on every Arsenal purchase. See [Rates & active referrals](../referral/rates.md).

**Is the leaderboard reset?**
No. It continues from Season 1: the top 100 by total ASTRO earned. See [Seasons & leaderboard](../seasons/overview.md).
