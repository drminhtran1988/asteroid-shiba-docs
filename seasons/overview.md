# Seasons & leaderboard

Asteroid Shiba is played in **seasons**. Each season has its own world and its own gameplay; the leaderboard runs through all of them.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Season 1 is complete.** **The Season 2 prelaunch — The Occupied Belt — is live now:** see [Season 2 at a glance](../season-2/overview.md).
{% endhint %}

## The leaderboard

The leaderboard is simple: explorers are ranked by **how much ASTRO they have earned**. The more ASTRO you earn, the higher you are. It works the same in Season 1 and Season 2.

```
Earn ASTRO → climb the leaderboard → top 100
```

## Leaderboard reward

The **$ASTEROID token has not been issued yet.** What the leaderboard does is decide who is standing in line when it is.

The **top 100 explorers** are added to the **token whitelist**, and **up to 2% of the future token emission** is reserved for that group — distributed by rank. The higher you finish, the bigger your share.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**That top 100 is decided across every season up to listing — not by any single season.** Season 1 is the first stage of that race, not the end of it. Nothing is locked in until listing.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
No token exists today and nothing is claimable yet. Exact per-rank allocations, token details, and claim &#38; vesting terms will be published with the tokenomics ahead of the token generation event (TGE).
{% endhint %}

## Rewards may change

The $ASTEROID tokenomics are **still in development**. Until they are final:

* **airdrops, leaderboard rewards and other allocations** — their size, form, eligibility and how they are distributed — **may change from season to season**, depending on the final tokenomics;
* every figure on this page is an **upper limit, not a guaranteed amount**;
* the terms that apply are the ones announced for the relevant season and published with the tokenomics.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Nothing in this documentation is a promise of a specific reward, amount or token value. Rewards are confirmed only when they are published with the final tokenomics.
{% endhint %}

## Season 2 and the leaderboard

Season 2 continues **the same leaderboard** — the same top 100, ranked by total ASTRO earned. Your place carries over from Season 1. CARGO does not count: it is workshop material, not ASTRO.
