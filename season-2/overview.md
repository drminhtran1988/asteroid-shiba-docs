## What changes

In Season 1 you found asteroids and kept them. The system was empty, so nobody stood in your way.

In Season 2 the asteroids are already **occupied**. Colonies of **Tenants** moved in first and work them for themselves. One Shiba cannot take an asteroid from a whole colony, so the game changes from ownership to **raids**:

```
SEASON 1   find an asteroid, keep it, earn from it
SEASON 2   fly in, fight through, take what you can, fly home
```

## The loop

> Shiba flies out on a schedule → up to three scenes on the asteroid → she brings home what fits in her backpack → the base turns it into **ASTRO** and **CARGO** → you upgrade her gear → she flies again.

You don't steer her by hand. You are the voice in her headset: you choose what she carries first, what the workshop builds next, and you keep an eye on her temper. In the morning you read her journal.
