# Payments & Withdrawals

Asteroid Shiba runs on two currencies, kept cleanly separate: **USDT** for paying, **ASTRO** for playing.

## What you pay with — USDT (BEP20)

[Arsenal](../season-2/arsenal.md) purchases — the Pistol and the Backpack — are bought **directly with USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)**. There's no balance to top up: you pay for an upgrade, you get it.

## What you earn — ASTRO

Everything Shiba brings home becomes **ASTRO** — the in-game currency you can withdraw. See [Loot: ASTRO & CARGO](../season-2/loot.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
**CARGO** is workshop material only: it can't be sold, withdrawn or exchanged for ASTRO.
{% endhint %}

## Withdrawing your earnings

When you're ready to cash out, your **ASTRO is converted to USDT (BEP20)** and sent to your BSC wallet. Every payout is reviewed before it is sent.

| Rule | |
| ---- | --- |
| **Minimum** | $10 per withdrawal |
| **Maximum** | $150 per withdrawal |
| **How often** | one withdrawal per 24 hours |
| **Open requests** | one at a time — a new request can be created only after the previous one is processed |
| **Processing time** | up to 72 hours |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Rate: 1 ASTRO = 0.001 USDT** — the same as in Season 1. **No withdrawal fees.**
{% endhint %}

## Free starter kit: $20 withdrawal limit

While you play on the **free starter kit (Pistol Lv0)**, you can withdraw **up to $20 in total**. Buying **Pistol Lv1** in the [Arsenal](../season-2/arsenal.md) removes the limit.

## In short

* **Pay** in USDT (BEP20) — for the Pistol and the Backpack in the Arsenal.
* **Earn** ASTRO — from Shiba's expeditions and referral commission.
* **Withdraw** — ASTRO → USDT (BEP20) to your wallet: $10–$150 per withdrawal, one per 24 hours, processed within 72 hours.
