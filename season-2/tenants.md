# The Tenants

Not beasts — a beast wants to eat. Everything living in these rocks was once worth something. The value evaporated; the habit stayed. And in a colony, every habit got a job: a storekeeper, a guard, a night shift at the bottom, someone checking papers.

There are dozens of kinds. A few you'll meet in her journal:

| Tenant | |
| ------ | --- |
| **PinkPepe** | Pink and gloomy. Sits there, sulks, blocks the way. |
| **Bagsitter** | The colony's storekeeper. Sits on an empty bag and won't say what's inside. |
| **Stillearly** | Sits at the very bottom. It's not stubbornness — it's his shift. |
| **Fossildoge** | Turned to stone and still happy: much rock. very ore. wow. |
| **Hamsterite** | An extinct species. Growths from 2024. |
| **Pudgeon** | A penguin in uniform. Wants to see your papers for the rock. |
| **Wencat** | Answers every question with a question: wen? |
| **Fudspitter** | Spreads rumours faster than gas spreads through the tunnels. |
| **Rugger** | Pulls the ground from under her paws. |
| **Jeetbug** | Bolts at the first rustle. |
| **Trencher** | Lives in the tunnels the colony dug. |
| **Mudeng** | Small, round and very unhappy. |
