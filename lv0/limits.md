# The Level 0 withdrawal limit

Level 0 accounts can withdraw up to **$25 in total, for the lifetime of the account**.

## What that means exactly

* You keep **earning** normally. Mining doesn't stop, Research still works, your balance keeps growing.
* You keep **playing** normally. Signals, hunting, storage — nothing is blocked.
* Only **cashing out** is capped. Once your withdrawals add up to $25, further withdrawal requests are declined while you're still at Level 0.

The cap counts **every withdrawal from the account**, whatever the ASTRO came from — asteroid mining, Research, or referral commission. It's a single lifetime total, not a daily or monthly allowance.

Your remaining allowance is shown on the withdrawal screen, so you always know where you stand.

## Who it applies to

**Every Level 0 account.** Paid entries, accounts that won their Scanner in a [Salvage Run](salvage-run.md), and Founder accounts alike. Level 0 is one tier with one set of rules.

## Why it exists

Level 0 is a demo tier, and its earnings come out of the same season pool as everyone else's. The cap keeps that pool from being drained by accounts that never move past the demo — so the players who do commit to the season still find something in it.

## Lifting it

Buy **Scanner Level 1**. The limit is a Level 0 rule; from Level 1 up, withdrawals are uncapped and follow the standard [payments and withdrawals](../payments/overview.md) terms.
