## Coming from Season 1

Your Scanner comes with you — and becomes your Pistol.

* **Your Pistol level = your Scanner level.** Scanner Lv6 becomes Pistol Lv6. Scanner Lv0 becomes Pistol Lv0, which follows the same 30-day rule as the starter kit below.
* **The Backpack is new.** Your first Arsenal purchase is **Backpack Lv1 (10 USDT)**; it unlocks the rest of the Arsenal.
* **The five slots** — Helmet, Suit, Scanner, Boots, Rope — you have from day one, like everyone. The base upgrades them with CARGO.

{% hint style="info" %}
A high Pistol with a Backpack Lv1 leaves part of the loot behind — for example, Pistol Lv10 with Backpack Lv1 delivers 55%. The Arsenal shows the share before you buy. See [The Arsenal](arsenal.md).
{% endhint %}

## New to Asteroid Shiba

New explorers get a **free starter kit**: **Pistol Lv0** and **Backpack Lv1**.

The kit works for **30 days**:

* received **before** Season 2 starts → 30 days from the start of Season 2;
* received **after** Season 2 starts → 30 days from the moment you receive it.

The 30-day limit is on **Pistol Lv0** only:

* after 30 days, a Pistol Lv0 stops working and expeditions pause until you buy **Pistol Lv1 — 50 USDT** (Pistol Lv0 has no credit value);
* the **Backpack stays yours** — nothing happens to it;
* with **Pistol Lv1 or higher** there is no time limit at all.

On the free kit you can withdraw **up to $20 in total**; Pistol Lv1 removes that limit too. See [Payments & Withdrawals](../payments/overview.md).
