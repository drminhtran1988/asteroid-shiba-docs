# Community Drop

**Community Drop** is an airdrop contest for the Season 2 prelaunch, held on X (Twitter).

## How to take part

1. Open our X account — [@AsteroidShibaHQ](https://x.com/AsteroidShibaHQ).
2. Find the **pinned post** at the top — [here it is](https://x.com/AsteroidShibaHQ/status/2102026059990380788).
3. Leave a reply under it with the **hashtag named in the pinned post**.

That's it — your reply with the hashtag is your entry.

{% hint style="info" %}
The hashtag, the rules, the prize and the dates are in the [pinned post](https://x.com/AsteroidShibaHQ/status/2102026059990380788). Only replies under that post count.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
We will never DM you first, and taking part never requires a wallet connection, a payment or your seed phrase. Official links only: [Community & links](../reference/links.md). Airdrop terms may change as the tokenomics are finalized — see [Rewards may change](../seasons/overview.md#rewards-may-change).
{% endhint %}
