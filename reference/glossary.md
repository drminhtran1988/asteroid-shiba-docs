# Glossary

**Signal** — one attempt to scan the field for an asteroid. You get 5 per day.

**Scan / Hunt** — spending a signal to search the void.

**Discovery** — the moment a scan reveals an asteroid; also the instant reward you get for finding it.

**Rarity** — an asteroid's class: Empty, Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, or Genesis.

**Scanner** — your account upgrade path; Level 0 is the paid entry tier, and each level from 1 up opens rarer tiers and raises the *probability* of finding them.

**Research** — spending ASTRO on a specific asteroid to raise its daily income (up to +50%). Burns ASTRO.

**Portfolio** — your collection of found asteroids that mine ASTRO for you (1 active earner at Level 0, up to 50 from Level 1).

**Buffer** — each asteroid's storage; fills up, then pauses until you Collect.

**ASTRO** — the in-game currency. 1 ASTRO = 0.001 USDT on withdrawal. Earned by finding and holding asteroids, spent on Research.

**Genesis** — the rarest, highest-value asteroid tier. Time-gated; reachable from a Level 1 Scanner up, never at Level 0.

**Leaderboard** — the top 100 explorers by total ASTRO earned, in every season.

**Storage** — asteroids found at Level 0 that are yours but not yet earning; they activate when you buy Scanner Level 1.

**Salvage Run** — the free draw held every two days, where one new explorer who has not started hunting yet wins a Scanner Level 1.

**Founder Shiba** — the free, exclusive mascot claimable only during the Founder Phase.

**Ambassador** — a manually granted profile with higher referral rates (10/6/3). Not purchasable.

**Active referral** — a referred friend who has spent $10+ (Scanner upgrades and Season 2 Arsenal purchases count).

**Upgrade commission** — the ASTRO you earn when your referrals buy Scanner upgrades or Season 2 Arsenal items (8/4/2 standard, 10/6/3 ambassador), paid on top by the game.

## Season 2

**The Belt (The Occupied Belt)** — the Season 2 star system. Its asteroids are already occupied.

**Tenants** — the colonies living on the Belt's asteroids. An asteroid can't be taken from them, only raided.

**Expedition** — one raid: flight, up to three scenes, the trip home. Five departures a day are in the schedule (UTC).

**Scene** — one moment on the asteroid: Clash, Air, Find, Passage or Ledge. Each kind tests one piece of gear.

**Setback** — a scene that didn't go through. The expedition ends, what's in the backpack is delivered, and the next departure is skipped. Shiba never dies.

**Core** — small, valuable loot. Becomes ASTRO at the base.

**Ore** — bulky loot. Becomes a little ASTRO and CARGO.

**CARGO** — workshop material. Can't be sold, withdrawn or exchanged for ASTRO.

**Pistol** — decides how much ASTRO every find is worth. Bought in the Arsenal.

**Backpack** — decides what share of the loot makes it home. Bought in the Arsenal.

**Arsenal** — where the Pistol and Backpack are upgraded, paid in USDT (BEP20). You pay only the difference to the next level.

**Workshop** — the base builds Helmet, Suit, Scanner, Boots and Rope stages from CARGO; up to fifteen upgrades a season, opened by calendar.

**Order** — Valuables or Material: what the backpack takes first. Takes effect at 00:00 UTC.

**The Bar** — if you don't open the game during a UTC day, Shiba spends 25–35% of that day's ASTRO there.
