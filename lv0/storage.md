# Asteroid storage

At Level 0 you can only have **one asteroid earning at a time**. But you never lose a find: everything else you discover goes into **storage** and waits for you.

## How it works

```
First Common   → active slot → earns ASTRO every day
Every find after → storage    → held for you, earns nothing yet
        ↓
Buy Scanner Lv1 → everything in storage becomes active and starts earning
```

Storage holds up to **49 asteroids**, on top of the one in your active slot.

## What a stored asteroid does — and doesn't

A stored asteroid is **really yours**. It is recorded against the season's supply, it carries its rarity, and nobody else can find that one. It shows in your Portfolio, greyed out, with a lock.

What it does not do, until you reach Level 1:

* It **earns no daily income**.
* It **cannot be collected**.
* It **cannot be researched**.
* Rare and Epic in storage pay **no instant discovery reward**.

That last point matters, so it's worth saying plainly: a Rare found at Level 1 pays 5,000 ASTRO the moment you find it. The same Rare found at Level 0 pays **nothing on discovery**. You keep the asteroid; you don't get the discovery reward for it.

Commons are the exception — a Common pays its 1,000 ASTRO instant reward every time, even when it goes to storage rather than the active slot.

## When you upgrade

Buying **Scanner Level 1** activates your whole storage at once. Your fleet cap goes from 1 to 50, so everything fits.

Income starts **from the moment you upgrade**, going forward. There is no back-pay: an Epic that sat in storage for forty days does not pay forty days of mining when it wakes up, and it does not pay its discovery reward retroactively. What you get is the asteroid, live, from that day on.

{% hint style="info" %}
Storage is why upgrading gets more valuable the longer you hunt at Level 0. Every Rare and Epic you find is a slot in the fleet you'll switch on the day you move up.
{% endhint %}
